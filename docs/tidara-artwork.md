## Final prompt

Use case: cinematic photographic background for Tidara, a premium coastal tide and reflection web app. Create a landscape 1536x1024 photographic seascape, quietly luxurious and natural, captured at blue hour on an anonymous open coast. Long low Atlantic swells, richly detailed dark teal ocean, subtle silver seafoam sweeping diagonally from lower right toward the centre, delicate haze separating sea and a wide distant horizon in the upper third. Very restrained warm champagne light peeking through thin distant clouds, no visible sun. The foreground water has beautiful physical texture, depth, translucent jade edges and soft blurred motion in the foam, never plastic, cartoonish or overly smooth. Restrained palette: deep midnight teal, charcoal blue, silver mist, tiny warm ivory reflections. Fine film grain, editorial travel photography, cinematic atmospheric perspective, calm and spacious. Keep the upper left and left centre especially dark, open and quiet for white text overlaid by the app. Wave detail and most visual interest on the right half and lower edge. The image should feel like standing above a secluded coast at the edge of night, not a generic stock beach sunset. No people, no buildings, no boats, no animals, no visible Moon, no stars, no text, no logos, no typography, no borders, no UI mockup. Photoreal ocean environment only, no graphic design.
